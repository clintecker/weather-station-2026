## 1. Snap off the back rails (before any soldering)

1. Score both faces along the line between column j and the inner back rail (card Y 41.91 mm from the front edge [TBD-MEASURE]: measure on the card), clamp the card along the line and snap the two back rails off.
2. File the edge flat and square to the front edge; stop at the line (do not open the column j holes).
3. Check: the card is 41.91 mm deep everywhere; it slides in a test slot without catching.

## Before power

- Meter each strip in the map to its neighbours: no continuity between neighbouring strips.
- No continuity between the 3.3 V supply and ground (both front rails).
- Header pin n (column j, row n) to backplane line n: check against `build/BACKPLANE.md`.
- No continuity from any battery lead (WAGO E1, shunt pads) to any strip.
