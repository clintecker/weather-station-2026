# B4 POWER: build sheet

`layout/drawings.py` writes this sheet from `layout/stack.py`. The drawing is `drawings/B4-top.svg`.

Hole names are a letter plus a number, as on the SolderFul silkscreen: `e10` is column E, row 10. SolderFul and `layout/stack.py` call the lettered lines A-J columns and the numbered lines 1-30 rows. Rail `F+` is the outer front rail and carries the 3.3 V supply. Rail `F-` is the inner front rail and carries ground. The front edge (column A) faces the lid, and the back edge plugs into the backplane.

Each strip on B4 carries one line, and `layout/layout_checks.py` checks that. Each wire follows its route in `drawings/B4-top.svg`. `layout/route.py` lays every route flat, in straight runs on the 1.27 mm grid, with right-angle bends around every part.

Lengths are cut lengths: the route plus 10 mm, rounded up to the next 5 mm. Use 24 AWG solid insulated wire unless the table says otherwise. Its insulation OD is about 1.45 mm `[TBD-MEASURE]`.

Insulation colours: 3.3 V red, ground black, I2C clock (SCL) yellow, I2C data (SDA) blue. The battery and charger lines (battery + junction, charger battery +, SYS) are red with a black heat-shrink band at each end. The battery sense leads are white with a red band. Every other signal is green, and bare jumpers are bare.

## 1. Snap off the back rails (before any soldering)

1. Score both faces along the line between column J and the inner back rail. That line is at card Y 41.91 mm from the front edge [TBD-MEASURE: measure it on your B4]. Clamp B4 along the line and snap the two back rails off.
2. File the edge flat and square to the front edge. Stop at the line, so you don't open the column J holes.
3. Check that B4 is 41.91 mm deep everywhere and slides into a test slot without catching.
4. Label it: a red paint-pen or sticker dot on the top face at the front edge, near row 1. That's the B4 colour, and the matching dot goes on the base beside the B4 slot (ASSEMBLY.md). The header's pin 30 comes out before you fit the header (the slot key, see the header in the parts list below), so B4 seats only in its own slot.

## Before power

- Meter each strip in the map to its neighbours: no continuity between neighbouring strips, except these pairs, which beep:
  - rows 19-20, columns F-J (joined by the i19-i20 jumper)
  - rows 20-21, columns F-J (through U2's shunt, X1 VIN- to X1 VIN+, 15 milliohms)
  - rows 21-22, columns F-J (joined by the i21-i22 jumper)
- No continuity between the 3.3 V supply and ground (the two front rails).
- Each header pin Jn beeps to Fn (column J to column F in the same row: its own strip). It beeps to no other header pin, except the groups below.
- Header pins J1, J24 beep to each other: all 3.3 V supply.
- Header pins J2, J12, J25 beep to each other: all ground.
- Header pins J19, J20, J21, J22 beep to each other: 19-20 and 21-22 by the top jumpers, and 20-21 through U2's shunt (X1 VIN- to X1 VIN+, 15 milliohms).
- The 1x30 header less its key pin: J30 pulled (hole j30 empty), and every other pin fitted. Spare pins (no net on B4, and their socket contacts sit on isolated pads): J27 (its strip also holds U7 shutdown, unused), J28, J29.
