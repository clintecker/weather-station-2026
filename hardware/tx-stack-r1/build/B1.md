# B1 PORTS A: build sheet

Generated by `layout/drawings.py` from `layout/stack.py`; drawing `drawings/B1-top.svg`. Holes as the SolderFul silkscreen names them: row letter + column number (`e10` = row E, column 10; letters A-J are rows, numbers 1-30 are columns); rails `F+` (outer front: 3.3 V supply) and `F-` (inner front: ground). The front edge (row A) faces the lid; the back edge plugs into the backplane. Every strip carries one line (checked by `layout/layout_checks.py`). Every wire follows its route in `drawings/{b}-top.svg` (`layout/route.py`: flat, straight runs on the 1.27 mm grid with right-angle bends, around every part). Lengths are cut lengths (route + 10 mm), 24 AWG solid insulated unless the table says otherwise (insulation OD about 1.45 mm, `[TBD-MEASURE]`). Insulation colours: 3.3 V red; ground black; I2C clock (SCL) yellow; I2C data (SDA) blue; the battery and charger lines (battery + junction, charger battery +, SYS) red with a black heat-shrink band at each end; the battery sense leads white with a red band; every other signal green. Bare jumpers are bare.

## 1. Snap off the back rails (before any soldering)

1. Score both faces along the line between row J and the inner back rail (card Y 41.91 mm from the front edge [TBD-MEASURE]: measure on the card), clamp the card along the line and snap the two back rails off.
2. File the edge flat and square to the front edge; stop at the line (do not open the row J holes).
3. Check: the card is 41.91 mm deep everywhere; it slides in a test slot without catching.

## Before power

- Meter each strip in the map to its neighbours: no continuity between neighbouring strips.
- No continuity between the 3.3 V supply and ground (both front rails).
- Header pin n (row J, column n) to backplane line n: check against `build/BACKPLANE.md`.
