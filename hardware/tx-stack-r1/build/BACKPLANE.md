# Backplane: build sheet

Generated by `layout/drawings.py`; drawing `drawings/backplane.svg`. A BusBoard PAD3U padboard (160.02 x 100.08 mm, 38 x 62 holes on 0.1 in, a separate plated pad per hole and no strips) cut to 21 x 39 holes. Nothing on it connects unless a wire does: on lines 1-18 a tinned bus wire on the copper side, soldered at every used pad, carries bus line n for every card. Lines run up and down in the box. Component side faces the cards (front); copper faces the back wall.

## 1. Cut and drill

1. Cut an L from the bottom-right corner of one PAD3U, seen from the face that becomes the front, so its factory edges are its right and bottom: 21 holes across by 39 holes along, plus a tab one hole wide (line -1) beside holes 1-3 at the bottom left. Score both faces midway between two rows of holes, not through them, and snap the long cuts; cut the inside corner of the L with a hacksaw (down between lines -1 and 0 to the score above hole 3), then sand every edge flat. The left edge above the tab (line 0) must end 0.5-1.2 mm from the near side of line 0's holes, or it will not fit the left wall's groove; the tab's left edge is midway between line -2 and line -1. Lines -1 (tab) and 0-20 across; holes 1-39 along.
2. Drill 3.2 mm through three pads, for the M3 screws to the base's three floor posts: line 0 hole 2, line 20 hole 2, line 20 hole 36 (bottom left, bottom right, top right). The tab leaves about 2.2 mm of board left of the line 0 hole. Line 0 has no screw higher up: its left edge sits in the left wall's groove. Nothing else is soldered on lines -1, 0 and 20.
3. Meter two neighbouring pads anywhere: open (the board has no strips).

## 6. Fit it in the base (`cage/backplane-mount-spec.md`)

1. Copper side back, mux behind it: lower the board onto the three posts 2.5 mm to the right of its place (toward the right wall), its copper face down on the post tops; its left edge is then clear of the left wall.
2. Slide it 2.5 mm left, flat on the posts, until its left edge is in the left wall's groove and the rail below it, down to just above the tab (it stops 0.5 short of the groove's floor), the tab lands on the bottom-left post, and the three drilled holes are over the posts' inserts. The mux moves with it and clears the bottom-left post's flat and the bottom-right post in both places.
3. Drive the three M3 x 6 button-head screws (ISO 7380, no washers). It comes out the same way, after every card is out.

- Each bus wire: continuity from its first to its last soldered hole.
