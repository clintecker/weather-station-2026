# Backplane: build sheet

Generated by `layout/drawings.py`; drawing `drawings/backplane.svg`. A BusBoard PAD3U padboard (160.02 x 100.08 mm, 38 x 62 holes on 0.1 in, a separate plated pad per hole and no strips) cut to 21 x 39 holes. Nothing on it connects unless a wire does: on lines 1-18 a tinned bus wire on the copper side, soldered at every used pad, carries bus line n for every card. Lines run up and down in the box. Component side faces the cards (front); copper faces the back wall.

## 1. Cut and drill

1. Cut a piece 21 holes across by 39 holes along from the bottom-right corner of one PAD3U, seen from the face that becomes the front, so its factory edges are its right and bottom (score both faces midway between two rows of holes, not through them, snap, sand the edges flat). The left edge (line 0) must end 0.5-1.2 mm from the near side of line 0's holes, or it will not fit the left wall's groove. Lines 0-20 across; holes 1-39 along.
2. Drill 3.2 mm through three pads, for the M3 screws to the base's three floor posts: line 0 hole 2, line 20 hole 2, line 20 hole 36 (bottom left, bottom right, top right). The top-left corner has no screw: the board's left edge sits in the left wall's groove. Nothing else is soldered on lines 0 and 20.
3. Meter two neighbouring pads anywhere: open (the board has no strips).

The mux (Adafruit #5626: bridge A0 for 0x71, its Vlogic switch SW1 to V+) sits on a foam pad on the copper side over holes 2-9, below the B4 socket and clear of the bus wires (they start at hole 10); its five header pads take short leads to hole 10.

## 6. Fit it in the base (`cage/backplane-mount-spec.md`)

1. Copper side back, mux behind it: lower the board onto the three posts 2.5 mm to the right of its place (toward the right wall), its copper face down on the post tops; its left edge is then clear of the left wall.
2. Slide it 2.5 mm left, flat on the posts, until its left edge is in the left wall's groove (it stops 0.5 short of the groove's floor) and the three drilled holes are over the posts' inserts. The mux moves with it and clears the bottom-left post's flat in both places.
3. Drive the three M3 x 6 button-head screws (ISO 7380, no washers). It comes out the same way, after every card is out.

- Each bus line to its neighbours: open. Line 2 to line 12: closed (both ground, joined at hole 38).
- 3.3 V supply (line 1) to ground (line 2): open.
- Each bus wire: continuity from its first to its last soldered hole.
