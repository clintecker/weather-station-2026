# Backplane: build sheet

Generated by `layout/drawings.py`; drawing `drawings/backplane.svg`. A BusBoard PAD3U padboard (160.02 x 100.08 mm, 38 x 62 holes on 0.1 in, a separate plated pad per hole and no strips): its 31 rows at the plated end, each the full 38 holes. Nothing on it connects unless a wire does: on lines 1-18 a bare bus wire on the copper side, soldered at every used pad, carries bus line n for every card (every socket is 1x30); lines 19-23 and 26 carry B4's power pins (the battery path, its senses and SYS) down to short leads to the charger and the pack, and the charger's own ground lead (J3 GND, SYS's return) lands on line 12, the bus ground. Lines run up and down in the box. Component side faces the cards (front); copper faces the back wall.

## 1. Cut and drill

1. Take one PAD3U and find its end with the two large plated mounting holes (the other end has two small plain holes). Count 31 rows in from that end: they become lines 30 (next to the mounting holes) down to 0, each the full 38 holes. Seen from the face that becomes the front (pads at the back), the mounting-hole end is the right edge. Keep the PAD3U's own right, top and bottom edges: no cut there.
2. Before cutting: drill line 0 hole 2 (the second hole from the bottom of line 0) 3.2 mm, for the M3 screw to the bottom-left post, and drill the lower mounting hole (level with hole 2) out from 2.8 to 3.2 mm for the M3 screw to the bottom-right post. Leave the upper mounting hole as it is: the right corner post's slot holds that corner.
3. Score both faces midway between line 0 and the next row out (never through the holes) and snap: the only cut. The score runs through the line 0 hole you drilled, so that hole opens 0.3 mm into the edge: that is expected, and the screw head clamps the board beside it. Sand the edge flat. It must end 1.27 mm from line 0's hole centres (0.5-1.2 from their near side), or it will not fit the left wall's groove.
4. Check the right edge: it should be 2.83 mm beyond the mounting holes' centres (5.48 from line 30's) [TBD-MEASURE: the scan cuts that end off]. The fit needs it no more than 6.13 past line 30's centres.
5. Meter two neighbouring pads anywhere: open (the board has no strips).

Fit each socket square to the board (a card pin bent sideways will not enter); solder one end pin, check, then the rest. Every socket is 1x30 (lines 1-30: its card's 29 header pins and, in the 30th contact, the key plug below; all 30 tails are soldered, each in its own pad). On lines 19-30 only B4's contacts on lines 19-23 and 26 meet a bus wire; every other contact there is soldered to its own pad and nothing else (an isolated pad): XB4 (hole 13) lines 24-25, 27-30; XB2 (hole 19) lines 19-30; XB1 (hole 27) lines 19-30; XB3 (hole 35) lines 19-30. Cut each from a longer strip by pulling the contact one past the end and cutting through that empty position (one position lost at each cut); file the cut face flat.

**Key plugs (the slot keys).** The four sockets are identical, so each gets a key plug that only its own card clears: XB4 (for B4) in contact 30, XB2 (for B2) in contact 28, XB1 (for B1) in contact 27, XB3 (for B3) in contact 29. Each card has that same pin pulled from its header (its build sheet); every other card still has it, stubs on the plug and cannot seat (B3 in B4's slot would put the battery on the Wio-E5). Make each plug from the generic male header stock: pull one pin out of the plastic, push its long end into the socket contact from the front until it bottoms, and cut it flush with the socket mouth (file the cut end flat; nothing may stand proud of the mouth). The plug is held by the contact's grip alone: never solder it, and nothing else is soldered to that contact's pad (it stays an isolated pad: no bus wire, no lead). After fitting, try each card in each wrong socket by hand, off the base: it must stop at the mouth.

**Colour dots.** A paint-pen or sticker dot beside each socket on the front face and on the base beside its card slot, the same colour as the dot on that card's front edge: B1 blue, B2 green, B3 yellow, B4 red.

## 6. Fit it in the base (`cage/backplane-mount-spec.md`)

1. Copper side back, mux behind it: tilt the board and push its factory right edge into the right corner post's slot, to the slot's end (Xb 92.30); its left edge is then clear of the left wall's face. Lower the left side onto the posts and pads, copper face down.
2. Slide it left, flat on the posts, until its left edge is in the left wall's groove and the rail below it and the two screw holes are over the posts' inserts (about 2.84 mm). The right edge stays 3.66 mm into the slot. The mux moves with it and clears the posts and pads.
3. Drive the two M3 x 6 button-head screws (ISO 7380, no washers): bottom left (line 0 hole 2) and bottom right (the lower mounting hole). The support pads under s20.2, s28.16, s24.31 have no screws. It comes out the same way, after every card is out.

## Before power

- Each bus line to its neighbours: open. Line 2 to line 12: closed (both ground, joined at hole 38).
- 3.3 V supply (line 1) to ground (line 2): open.
- Lines 19-30: each B4 contact on lines 19-23 and 26 beeps to its line's bus wire (19 to 20, 21 to 22); every other contact on lines 19-30 (the isolated pads, the key plugs' included) beeps to nothing: not its line's wire, not its neighbours.
- The charger ground lead's EVM end (J3 GND) beeps to line 12 and line 2.
- Each bus wire: continuity from its first to its last soldered hole.
