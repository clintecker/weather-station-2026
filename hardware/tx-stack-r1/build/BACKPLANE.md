# Backplane: build sheet

Generated by `layout/drawings.py`; drawing `drawings/backplane.svg`. A BusBoard ST2 stripboard (100 x 80 mm, 31 strips x 39 holes, strips along the 100 mm side) cut to 21 strips x 39 holes. Strips run up and down in the box; strip n carries bus line n for every card. Component side faces the cards (front); copper faces the back wall.

## 1. Cut and drill

1. Cut 21 strips wide (strips 0-20). Keep all 39 holes along the strips.
2. Drill 3.2 mm at strips 0 and 20, about 4 mm in from each end, for the four M3 screws to the cage posts.
3. Do not cut any strip: every bus line runs the full length.

## Before power

- Each strip to its neighbours: open. Strip 2 to strip 12: closed (both ground).
- 3.3 V supply (strip 1) to ground (strip 2): open.
