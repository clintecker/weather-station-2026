# B2 PORTS B + METERS: build sheet

`layout/drawings.py` writes this sheet from `layout/stack.py`. The drawing is `drawings/B2-top.svg`.

Hole names are a letter plus a number, as on the SolderFul silkscreen: `e10` is column E, row 10. SolderFul and `layout/stack.py` call the lettered lines A-J columns and the numbered lines 1-30 rows. Rail `F+` is the outer front rail and carries the 3.3 V supply. Rail `F-` is the inner front rail and carries ground. The front edge (column A) faces the lid, and the back edge plugs into the backplane.

Each strip on B2 carries one line, and `layout/layout_checks.py` checks that. Each wire follows its route in `drawings/B2-top.svg`. `layout/route.py` lays every route flat, in straight runs on the 1.27 mm grid, with right-angle bends around every part.

Lengths are cut lengths: the route plus 10 mm, rounded up to the next 5 mm. Use 24 AWG solid insulated wire unless the table says otherwise. Its insulation OD is about 1.45 mm `[TBD-MEASURE]`.

Insulation colours: 3.3 V red, ground black, I2C clock (SCL) yellow, I2C data (SDA) blue. The battery and charger lines (battery + junction, charger battery +, SYS) are red with a black heat-shrink band at each end. The battery sense leads are white with a red band. Every other signal is green, and bare jumpers are bare.

## 1. Snap off the back rails (before any soldering)

1. Score both faces along the line between column J and the inner back rail. That line is at card Y 41.91 mm from the front edge [TBD-MEASURE: measure it on your B2]. Clamp B2 along the line and snap the two back rails off.
2. File the edge flat and square to the front edge. Stop at the line, so you don't open the column J holes.
3. Check that B2 is 41.91 mm deep everywhere and slides into a test slot without catching.
4. Label it: a green paint-pen or sticker dot on the top face at the front edge, near row 1. That's the B2 colour, and the matching dot goes on the base beside the B2 slot (ASSEMBLY.md). The header's pin 28 comes out before you fit the header (the slot key, see the header in the parts list below), so B2 seats only in its own slot.

## 3. Bare jumpers on top

Jumper 4 (a14 to F+14) runs straight over hole F-14 on the ground rail, and that hole has a bare pad. Sleeve this jumper (or use insulated wire), or arch it clear of F-14. Otherwise it can short the 3.3 V supply to ground.

## Before power

- Meter each strip in the map to its neighbours: no continuity between neighbouring strips, except rows 25-26, columns A-E (both ground, joined by the d25-d26 jumper), which beep.
- No continuity between the 3.3 V supply and ground (the two front rails).
- Each header pin Jn beeps to Fn (column J to column F in the same row: its own strip). It beeps to no other header pin, except the groups below.
- Header pins J2, J12 beep to each other: all ground.
- The 1x30 header less its key pin: J28 pulled (hole j28 empty), and every other pin fitted. Spare pins (no net on B2, and their socket contacts sit on isolated pads): J19, J20, J21, J22, J23, J24, J25, J26, J27, J29, J30.
