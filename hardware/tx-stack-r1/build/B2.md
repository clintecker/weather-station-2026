# B2 PORTS B + METERS: build sheet

Generated by `layout/drawings.py` from `layout/stack.py`; drawing `drawings/B2-top.svg`. Holes as the SolderFul silkscreen names them: row letter + column number (`e10` = row E, column 10; letters A-J are rows, numbers 1-30 are columns); rails `F+` (outer front: 3.3 V supply) and `F-` (inner front: ground). The front edge (row A) faces the lid; the back edge plugs into the backplane. Every strip carries one line (checked by `layout/layout_checks.py`). Every wire follows its route in `drawings/{b}-top.svg` (`layout/route.py`: flat, straight runs on the 1.27 mm grid with right-angle bends, around every part). Lengths are cut lengths (route + 10 mm), 24 AWG solid insulated unless the table says otherwise (insulation OD about 1.45 mm, `[TBD-MEASURE]`). Insulation colours: 3.3 V red; ground black; I2C clock (SCL) yellow; I2C data (SDA) blue; the battery and charger lines (battery + junction, charger battery +, SYS) red with a black heat-shrink band at each end; the battery sense leads white with a red band; every other signal green. Bare jumpers are bare.

## 1. Snap off the back rails (before any soldering)

1. Score both faces along the line between row J and the inner back rail (card Y 41.91 mm from the front edge [TBD-MEASURE]: measure on the card), clamp the card along the line and snap the two back rails off.
2. File the edge flat and square to the front edge; stop at the line (do not open the row J holes).
3. Check: the card is 41.91 mm deep everywhere; it slides in a test slot without catching.
4. Label it: a green paint-pen or sticker dot on the top face at the front edge, near column 1 (the B2 colour; the matching dot goes on the base beside the B2 slot, ASSEMBLY.md). The header's pin 28 comes out before fitting (the slot key, the header in the parts list below), so B2 seats only in its own slot.

## Before power

- Meter each strip in the map to its neighbours: no continuity between neighbouring strips.
- No continuity between the 3.3 V supply and ground (both front rails).
- Each header pin Jn beeps to Fn (row J to row F, same column: its own strip) and to no other header pin, except the groups below.
- Header pins J2, J12 beep to each other: all ground.
- The 1x30 header less its key pin: J28 pulled (hole j28 empty), every other pin fitted. Spare pins (no net on the card; their socket contacts sit on isolated pads): J19, J20, J21, J22, J23, J24, J25, J26, J27, J29, J30.
