# Assembly: charger set-up, the printed base, shelves, cards, cables

Generated by `layout/drawings.py`. Read with `drawings/cage-side.svg`, `build/B1..B4.md` and `build/BACKPLANE.md`.

## 1. Charger (BQ25798EVM) before any power: jumper changes (TI SLUUCB5; issue #34)

The EVM ships set for two cells. Do these in order, with nothing connected:

1. **JP24 off, JP22 on** (PROG: one cell, 1.5 MHz). JP22 is the only PROG jumper fitted.
2. **Battery sense wire first, then JP9 off.** Solder the charger battery sense lead (from R7, whose other leg is in the WAGO E1) to J5 SNS_BATP; only then remove JP9, so BATP is never left floating.
3. **JP18 off, thermistor on J7.** Fit the Adafruit #372 10 kohm NTC (B 3950) to J7 and tape it to the pack. The EVM's TS divider is set for a 103AT (B 3435): the cold and hot thresholds move by a few degrees; read the TS percentage over I2C at room temperature and confirm it sits in the normal band before charging.
4. **JP12, JP17 and JP30 stay in.** JP30 keeps the I2C pull-ups (R39-R42, 10 kohm) on the EVM's 3.3 V pull-up rail: they are counted in the bus total (1.40 kohm, schematic check 3).
5. Status LEDs: leave them, or remove their jumpers if fitted (about 1 mA); identify them on the board against SLUUCB5 before cutting anything.
6. USB in is J6, micro-B (JP7 on VIN1): use a USB-C to micro-B cable from the 2 A adapter.
7. **First power without the pack and with JP17 out** (charge disabled); over I2C read REG0x0A (cell count) and REG0x01-0x02 (charge voltage 4.2 V), and set the charge current (ICHG) to about 0.9 A (electrical review m6: the JP22 note says 2 A, the datasheet default is 1 A). Then refit JP17 and connect the pack.

## 2. Print the base (`cage/cage.py`)

1. Print the rim test ring first (`cage.py --ring`, `cage/stl/rim-ring.stl`: the whole rim, 175.5 x 129.5 x 8, about an hour, flat on its land) and press the real lid onto it: the lip must drop into the O-ring channel all the way round and through all four S-curves, all four holes under the lid's at once, the outer edge flush on all four sides. Correct `cage/lid-rim-spec.md` until it does, then set `ring_fit` True. Settle L1 (the screws, the lid's corner pocket); `cage.py --stl` refuses the full base until both are sourced. Also the EVM's part heights and J6 under it (`evm`, `evm_under`).
2. Then a second test coupon: one card slot (tune `slot_clear` until a card slides with light friction), one M4 insert post, one lid corner of the land and tongue (fit the lid on it: the sealing lip must press the O-ring about 1 mm, with the lid's walls down on the land), and a 30 mm square of the 3 mm wall to leak-test.
3. Print the base in PETG, a light colour, rim down on the plate (the O-ring land is the bed face), walls 3 mm (about 7 perimeters, solid). Set the M4 heat-set inserts in the four corner posts (option b: M4 stainless screws on 12 mm washers through the lid's 8 mm holes) and the M3 inserts in the backplane posts with the iron. Leak-test the bare base (water in the base, or soapy water with the lid on and a little pressure through the vent hole); coat the inside only if it leaks. Paint or clear-coat the outside against UV.
4. Fit the glands in the bottom wall's bosses (six 12.0 mm holes for PG-7s #762, the round port cables; two 15.5 mm for PG-9s #761, the flat wind and rain cables, right column), the M12 vent, and the SMA bulkhead in the right wall's boss. Fit the backplane (its build sheet, section 6): lowered onto its three posts 2.5 mm right of its place, slid left into the left wall's groove, three M3 screws; it comes off again only after every card is out.
5. Shelves bottom to top: the pack on its tray (strap); the EVM (Bumpon feet peeled off) slid back on its ledges to the stop, clip at the front, leads with service loops; then the cards.
6. Cables: each round port cable through its PG-7, cap tightened. Each flat weather-meter cable through a PG-9 with its seal out. Seal after the final cable position is set (silicone or a split grommet in the gap); the cable is then not removable without cutting the seal. Inside, a service loop in the space in front of the cards; outside, a drip loop below each gland before the cable is tied to the pole.
7. The coax: AD1 on the mini's SMA, its arm toward the lid; the 135110 plug on it, the cable bent right in front of the cards to the bulkhead; clip it to the right corner post at the bend.
8. Mount: hose clamps or U-bolts through the two pole lugs on the back; the lid on with its four screws.

## 3. Cards, bottom to top: B4 POWER, B2 PORTS B + METERS, B1 PORTS A, B3 CORE (top)

Every card has its back rails snapped off and the edge filed flat first (step 1 of its build sheet). Each card slides in from the lid side along its left-wall slot and right-post slot until its header pins enter the socket (4.96 mm; the header insulator meets the socket 0.84 mm further in). On B1 and B2 the back breakout's spacer tongue enters the right post's slot. Fit the retention bar (front left, two M3 screws) last. To take a card out: bar off, unplug that card's cables (jacks, battery leads on B4, the coax on B3), slide it out.

Battery leads: 24 AWG stranded (red +, black -), soldered at the boards and clamped in the WAGO E1 (strip 9-10 mm; about 8.4 mV drop per 0.1 m at 1 A, 84.2 mohm/m); sense leads after R7 and R8: 24 AWG stranded; nothing of the battery loop goes through a strip, a header or a socket (layout check L4). Leave 60-80 mm service loops on the jack cables and the solar lead inside the box, with a drip loop below each gland outside.

## 5. Before the first power

- Charger jumpers as in step 1; the pack disconnected; JP17 out.
- Each card's own 'Before power' list, and the backplane's.
- 3.3 V supply to ground on the backplane: open (more than 1 kohm).
