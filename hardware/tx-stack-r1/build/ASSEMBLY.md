# Assembly: charger set-up, the printed base, shelves, cards, cables

Generated by `layout/drawings.py`. Read with `drawings/cage-side.svg`, `build/B1..B4.md` and `build/BACKPLANE.md`.

## 1. Charger (BQ25798EVM) before any power: jumper changes (TI SLUUCB5; issue #34)

The EVM ships set for two cells. Do these in order, with nothing connected:

1. **JP24 off, JP22 on** (PROG: one cell, 1.5 MHz). JP22 is the only PROG jumper fitted.
2. **Battery sense wire first, then JP9 off.** Solder the charger battery sense lead (from R7 on the backplane, its free leg at line 24 hole 7) to J5 SNS_BATP; only then remove JP9, so BATP is never left floating.
3. **JP18 off, thermistor on J7.** Fit the Adafruit #372 10 kohm NTC (B 3950) to J7 and tape it to the pack. The EVM's TS divider is set for a 103AT (B 3435): the cold and hot thresholds move by a few degrees; read the TS percentage over I2C at room temperature and confirm it sits in the normal band before charging.
4. **JP12, JP17 and JP30 stay in.** JP30 keeps the I2C pull-ups (R39-R42, 10 kohm) on the EVM's 3.3 V pull-up rail: they are counted in the bus total (1.40 kohm, schematic check 3).
5. Status LEDs: leave them, or remove their jumpers if fitted (about 1 mA); identify them on the board against SLUUCB5 before cutting anything.
6. USB in is J6, micro-B (JP7 on VIN1): use a USB-C to micro-B cable from the 2 A adapter.
7. **First power without the pack and with JP17 out** (charge disabled); over I2C read REG0x0A (cell count) and REG0x01-0x02 (charge voltage 4.2 V), and set the charge current (ICHG) to about 0.9 A (electrical review m6: the JP22 note says 2 A, the datasheet default is 1 A). Then refit JP17 and connect the pack.

## 2. Print the base (`cage/cage.py`)

1. Print the rim test ring first (`cage.py --ring`, `cage/stl/rim-ring.stl`: the whole rim, 175.5 x 129.5 x 8, about an hour, flat on its land) and press the real lid onto it: the lip must drop into the O-ring channel all the way round and through all four S-curves, all four holes under the lid's at once, the outer edge flush on all four sides. Correct `cage/lid-rim-spec.md` until it does, then set `ring_fit` True. Settle L1 (the screws, the lid's corner pocket); `cage.py --stl` refuses the full base until both are sourced. Also the EVM's part heights and J6 under it (`evm`, `evm_under`).
2. Then a second test coupon: one card slot (tune `slot_clear` until a card slides with light friction), one M4 insert post, one lid corner of the land and tongue (fit the lid on it: the sealing lip must press the O-ring about 1 mm, with the lid's walls down on the land), and a 30 mm square of the 3 mm wall to leak-test.
3. Print the base in PETG, a light colour, rim down on the plate (the O-ring land is the bed face), walls 3 mm (about 7 perimeters, solid). Set the M4 heat-set inserts in the four corner posts (option b: M4 stainless screws on 12 mm washers through the lid's 8 mm holes) and the retention bar's two M3 inserts in the left wall's front face with the iron. Leak-test the bare base (water in the base, or soapy water with the lid on and a little pressure through the vent hole); coat the inside only if it leaks. Paint or clear-coat the outside against UV.
4. Fit the glands in the bottom wall's bosses (five 13 mm holes for PG-7s #762: the four sensor-port cables S1-S4 and the solar lead; two 15.5 mm for PG-9s #761, the flat wind and rain cables, right column; no spare), the M12 vent, and the SMA bulkhead low in the right wall (enclosure 4: Yb 94.14, Zb 60.38). Fit the backplane (its build sheet, section 6): its factory right edge tilted into the right corner post's slot, lowered and slid left into the left wall's groove; no screws, the cards hold it, so fit them straight after; it comes off again only after every card is out.
5. Shelves bottom to top: the pack on its tray (strap); the EVM (Bumpon feet peeled off) slid back on its ledges to the stop, clip at the front, leads with service loops; then the cards.
6. Cables. A 6P plug (9.65 x 6.6 mm, 11.7 across its corners, 12.2 with the latch) passes no gland: the PG-7 bore is 7.9 and the PG-9's 10.5 even with the seal out. So every cable goes through its gland with no plug on its inside end, and that plug is crimped after, inside the box (Clint's 6P crimp plugs; a 6P (RJ11/RJ12) crimp tool is needed). Round port cables: slide the cap, the seal and the body's bore over the bare end from outside, pull the length through, crimp the plug (same pin order as the module end: 2 3.3 V, 3 data, 4 ground, 5 clock), tighten the cap. The weather meters' flat cables come with their plugs fitted: note which colour sits at which plug position (wind 2-5, rain 3-4), cut the plug off outside the box, pass the cable in through the PG-9 with its seal out, crimp a new plug in the same order, then seal the gap (silicone or a split grommet) once the cable's position is final; the cable then comes out only by cutting the seal (and the plug). Check that the crimp plugs hold the meters' narrower flat cable with each conductor on its position (otherwise use 6P4C plugs). Inside, a service loop in the space in front of the cards; outside, a drip loop below each gland before the cable is tied to the pole. Each cable runs from its plug to the right column, down it and left under the pack tray to its gland (cage/shelves-spec.md).
7. The coax: AD1 on the mini's SMA, its arm toward the lid; the 135110 plug on it, the cable bent right in front of the cards and down the right column to the bulkhead low in the right wall; clip it to the right corner post. The 6 in cable reaches (Clint, 2026-11-17: it fits the low bulkhead with no tight bend). [TBD-MEASURE] Check the straight plug on the bulkhead's inner end clears the EVM's lid-right corner (or use a right-angle plug).
8. Mount: hose clamps or straps through the two slots in each mounting flange (the short ends, in the floor plane); the lid on with its four screws.

## 3. Cards, bottom to top: B4 POWER, B2 PORTS B + METERS, B1 PORTS A, B3 CORE (top)

**Labels (the slot key's colour code):** a dot on each card's front edge and the same colour beside its slot on the base and beside its socket on the backplane: B1 blue, B2 green, B3 yellow, B4 red. Match the dots. The key plugs (BACKPLANE.md section 2) make a wrong card stop at the socket mouth: if a card will not go the last few millimetres, it is in the wrong slot; never force it.

Every card has its back rails snapped off and the edge filed flat first (step 1 of its build sheet). Each card slides in from the lid side along its left-wall slot and right-post slot until its header pins enter the socket (4.96 mm; the header insulator meets the socket 0.84 mm further in). On B1 and B2 the RJ11 jacks overhang the front edge, mouths toward the lid. Fit the retention bar (front left, two M3 screws) last. To take a card out: bar off, unplug that card's own plugs (the RJ11 cables on B1 and B2, the coax at AD1 on B3, the SWD and USB-C leads if fitted), slide it out. No card needs another card out first, and nothing is soldered between a card and anything else (layout check L10).

| U1 system ground (J3 GND) | backplane line 12 hole 11 | 100 mm, trim to fit | charger system ground (J3 GND) to line 12 (the bus ground, SYS's return), 24 AWG stranded, black |

| EVM J8 (KK 4-pin housing, pins 1-3) | backplane charger pins | 150 mm | charger I2C: ground, clock, data |
| mux channel 0-3 (QT) | backplane QT lead pins | 150 mm (Adafruit #4209) | sensor ports S1-S4 |

Battery leads: 24 AWG stranded (red +, black -), soldered at the backplane and screwed at the EVM (about 8.4 mV drop per 0.1 m at 1 A, 84.2 mohm/m); the charger sense lead from R7: 24 AWG stranded. SYS (J3 SYS to line 26) and its return (J3 GND to line 12, black) are a pair of 24 AWG stranded leads of the same length, run side by side: the 3.3 V regulator's load and the radio's transmit peaks return on that ground lead, not through the I2C lead's J8 pin 1. The battery current reaches B4 only on doubled header pins (19-20, 21-22; layout check L4); no lead goes to a card (L10). Leave 60-80 mm service loops on the jack cables and the solar lead inside the box, with a drip loop below each gland outside.

## 5. Before the first power

- Charger jumpers as in step 1; the pack disconnected; JP17 out.
- Each card's own 'Before power' list, and the backplane's.
- 3.3 V supply to ground on the backplane: open (more than 1 kohm).
