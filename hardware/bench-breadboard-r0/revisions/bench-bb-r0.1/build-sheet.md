## 2. Jumpers

Each jumper is one wire between the two holes: pre-cut solid 22 AWG laid flat (record H3). One exception: the OLED's signal jumpers from board C to CN10 (W20, W21, W22, W23, W24) are female-to-male Dupont jumpers of about 30 cm on the morpho's male pins, bench OLED only (record N3; the OLED wires bench check). Another: the station signals still on the morpho's male pins (W15 (CUPS, CN10.14), W26 (RAIN, CN10.29)) are female-to-male Dupont jumpers too, the female end on the pin: a solid wire cannot be pushed onto a male pin. A later revision moves them to the ARDUINO female headers as solid 22 AWG (record 4.5, change B; the morpho signal pins bench check). Colour by class: i2c blue, wind green, rain cyan, charger orange, display purple, power red, ground black, analog pink, battery lead brown. A link is a jumper the model names by a stable id.

## 7. Mode A and mode B

Mode A (daily, the NUCLEO on USB) is drawn: the BATTERY POWER shunt open, the 100 ohm load fitted, the OLED on its own supply pair. A mode change never touches ground.

**Mode A to B:**

1. Unplug the NUCLEO's USB.
2. Open the load's two levers (WAGO_SYS_OUT position 2 and WAGO_STAR position 3) and take the 100 ohm load out.
3. Unplug the OLED's VCC lead (L_OLED_VCC) from CN7.16.
4. Fit the BATTERY POWER shunt (SHUNT).

**Mode B to A:** the reverse, the shunt out before the USB goes in.

1. Take the BATTERY POWER shunt out.
2. Plug the OLED's VCC lead (L_OLED_VCC) back into CN7.16.
3. Refit the 100 ohm load and close its two levers.
4. Plug the NUCLEO's USB in.

The TIE (L_TIE) and the CN6.7 lead (L_NUC_RET) never move.

## Power checks

The phase A power checks (spec `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-29-power-telemetry-design.md` section 11.1, amended by the build-progression record), in this order, with the section 6 rows each shares. **The model first:** a measured or fitted value goes into `source/wiring.toml` before the firmware, because D3 checks 8 and 9 (`make.py sheet`) hold the firmware to the model: check 2's R_ISET as the charger's `iset_ohms`; R4-R6 each measured (record 4.4 step 12) as their `measured_ohms`; the M2 ISET gain fit (4.4 step 11) as the charger's `iset_gain_ppm`. Then the same value into `firmware/stage0-node/App/power_config.h` (`POWER_R_ISET_OHM`, `POWER_VIN_DIVIDER_PPM`, `POWER_ISET_GAIN_PPM`), benchlink's `VIN_DIVIDER_PPM` and `tools/benchlink_log.py`'s `R_ISET_OHM` and `ISET_GAIN`, and regenerate this sheet, in one commit. Check 3's value goes in only if the shunt fails its check. Commit a measured value before flashing the build that carries it: a build from a dirty tree reports firmware build ID 0, "not identified" (spec section 8.6).

1. **Before power, no VBUS lead fitted** (pre-run check C1): on the INA228, X1.2 (VBUS) to X1.3 (VIN+) and X1.2 to X1.1 (VIN-) read open (SJ1, the back VBUS-to-VIN+ jumper, open). Then fit the L_VBUS sense lead into the OUT joint (WAGO_SYS_OUT position 3).
2. **R_ISET** (C16): with the charger unpowered and the pack unplugged, measure ISET to GND: R9, 1 kOhm expected (POWER_R_ISET_OHM 1000), behind the closed 1A jumper (KISET 890 A*ohm gives about 0.89 A). Any other reading replaces the charger's `iset_ohms` in the model first, then `POWER_R_ISET_OHM` and the logger's `R_ISET_OHM` (see above). The charge current read from ISET is I = 400 x V_ISET / R_ISET: the ISET monitor is 1/400 of the charge current, +-10 % (SLUS810 section 9.3.5.1), plus R_ISET's own tolerance.
3. **Shunt** (C5): verified, not measured (spec section 8.6, record M1): at a steady current the INA228 battery current agrees within 2 % + 2 mA with the Fluke 87V in series (its A input for about 1 A, its mA input, 400 mA, for the 38 mA load), spliced into the pack + lead at CELL+ position 1 (its two leads clipped to short 22 AWG solid stubs: the pack red lead and one stub in a spare 222-413, the other stub in position 1; the pack unplugged while it goes in and out), only for a short steady window: its current shunt adds tens to hundreds of mOhm, so never during a termination run or the battery-path checks (7, C12-C14). Only if the shunt fails, the reference-derived value (the meter's mV across the shunt's Kelvin points over the series A, never an ohm range) replaces the nominal 15 mOhm (1 %).
4. **ISET and VIN_SENSE before the pack** (W19 and W28): with the wall adapter on and the pack unplugged, VIN_SENSE (CN8.3; alias CN7.32) x the measured ratio (POWER_VIN_DIVIDER_PPM / 1e6, from R4-R6 each measured and held in the model as `measured_ohms`; record M2) agrees with the meter on VBUS, within 1 %, and ISET (CN8.1; alias CN7.28) reads near 0 V with no charge current.
5. **TS:** with input power present, THERM to GND reads about 0.75 V at 25 C (the 10 kOhm NTC at 75 uA). About 0.375 V means the breakout's fixed 10 kOhm TS resistor (R10, the THERM jumper) is still in parallel: cut it.
6. **CHG and PGOOD released** (W14 and W25; C17): with the charge done, and with the input removed for a moment, with the firmware's internal pull-ups on, PB10 (CHG: CN9.7; alias CN10.25) and PB8 (PGOOD: CN9.6; alias CN10.27) both read at or below min(VDD, VDDA) + 0.3 V (DS13293 table 26 note 3: above that the internal pull-ups must be off; record N4). The LED anodes are on OUT (schematic), so a released pin sits near OUT - Vf: 2.9 V measured on 2026-10-28/29, under the VDD + 0.3 V rule (3.6 V). Re-measure at each step.
7. **Battery path** (C12-C14, section 5): in the first 10 minutes of a CC charge, with the INA228 current I between 0.8 and 1.0 A, each span a mV drop on the meter over I (spec section 11.2, record P8): the + span (PATH_POS: pack plug +, back-probed, to the charger's header LIPO, including the 15 mOhm shunt) at most 75 mOhm; the - span (PATH_NEG: header GND to pack plug -, back-probed) at most 40 mOhm; each pack-side span (PATH_PACK_POS: pack plug + to the CELL+ clamp; PATH_PACK_NEG: the STAR clamp to pack plug -) at most 28 mOhm, their sum recorded as R_pack; each JST PH mating at most 20 mOhm; each lever clamp to clamp and each screw at most 5 mOhm. The leads of PATH_POS and PATH_NEG are about 37 mOhm of copper (L_PACK_POS, L_CELL_INA, L_INA_BATT, L_BATT_POS, L_BATT_NEG, L_PACK_NEG, from their gauge and length). A 0.2 ohm breadboard path ended 1 A charges early on 2026-10-28.
8. **Gauge** (C10, C14; step 1 exit 5, record R-2): at rest or at the 38 mA load, the gauge VCELL within 10 mV of the meter at the pack plug. At a 1 A charge, the gauge VCELL within 10 mV of the meter at the gauge's own sense points (the CELL+ clamp to the STAR clamp), and the pack-plug difference (gauge VCELL - the meter at the pack plug) recorded, at or below I x R_pack + 10 mV (R_pack from C14).
9. **INA VBUS = OUT, and SJ1 really open:** during a charge with the pack below 4.1 V, OUT (`load_mv`, the INA228 VBUS) reads 4.3-4.5 V and at least 100 mV above the gauge VCELL. A closed SJ1 ties the cell to OUT and fails this check.
10. **TMR and termination** (record P9): with the charger unpowered, TMR reads 0 ohm to GND (the schematic ties it to GND: all safety timers disabled, so the charge may start from any cell voltage). A charge at the 1A jumper then ends with CHG off and steady, not flashing, while the INA228 battery current is 0.20-0.40 A (ITERM about 0.3 A) and the cell (gauge VCELL - I x R_pack) is at 4.12 V or more. Flashing at about 2 Hz means expired safety timers (SLUS810 table 9-2): with TMR at GND it should not happen, so check TMR and the CHG lead. If the board in hand has TMR floating (default 4-6 h), record it and start the charge at 3.85 V or more; no RTMR is fitted (record P9).

## Step 1b power checks

Step 1b, after the step 2 H01 run, on the step 2 plate's small breadboard (record S1, 7.1 Q8), before the node runs profile B (`WS_POWER_PROFILE=2`): the load, solar and USB-C INA228s and the board's own D2/D4 OR pair (record P10). Step 1b is not drawn on this sheet: its wiring belongs to the protoboard model (record D7). Checks 1-10 still hold.

B1. **EN1 and EN2** (charger unpowered): record both solder jumpers of the #4755 again, SJ2 (EN1) and SJ1 (EN2), as the step 1 bench check "Charger jumpers" first did. The design needs **EN2 high and EN1 low**: the R_ILIM input limit, no VIN-DPM (SLUS810 table 7-2). EN1 = EN2 = high is standby, no charge. EN2 low means a 100 or 500 mA input limit with VIN-DPM: stop and record it (record 1.4, P10).
B2. **Addresses:** the i2cscan bus scan lists 0x40 battery, 0x41 load, 0x45 solar and 0x48 USB-C (`power_config.h` `POWER_ADDR_*`), each with an `#rail` line (0x41, 0x45, 0x48). 0x48 is the ADDR1-to-SDA wire, allowed as INA228 module preparation (7.1 Q1): scope SDA against SCL on this bus, data hold at least 100 ns after the address MSB (spec R15).
B3. **Rail shunts:** verified, not measured, as check 3: at a steady current on each rail (the load INA228 with the 100 ohm load; the solar and USB-C INA228s each during a charge from that source alone), the INA228 current (the i2cscan `#rail` line, or the node's `load_current_ma`, `solar_current_ma`, `usb_current_ma`) agrees within 2 % + 2 mA with the Fluke 87V in series in that rail's + lead at its lever joint, the same short steady window as check 3. Only a rail that fails gets its reference-derived value in `power_config.h` (`POWER_SHUNT_UOHM_LOAD`, `_SOLAR`, `_USB`).
B4. **Bus pull-up** (exit f): bus idle and unpowered, SDA to 3V3 and SCL to 3V3 each read 0.97 kOhm or more, the measured value recorded (about nine 10 kOhm boards in parallel; power-metering.md section 7). Bus idle and powered, SDA high reads 2.8 V or more (the 0x48 board's 10 kOhm ADDR1 pull-down stays on SDA; record 1.4).
B5. **One source at a time** (exit e): never solar and USB-C together. With solar alone (the wall adapter unplugged at the mains end), the USB-C INA228 reads |I| <= 1 mA and, with a 10 kOhm test load across the idle USB-C input, that input reads <= 0.5 V (D2 blocking); then the same with USB-C alone and the panel unplugged at its adapter: the solar INA228 reads |I| <= 1 mA and the idle solar input, with the same 10 kOhm load, <= 0.5 V (D4 blocking). The live source's INA228 VBUS reads the source, and `charger_input_mv` reads it less one Schottky drop (0.35-0.5 V).
B6. **A weak source with EN2 high** (power-metering.md section 5.4): a bench supply at 7 V with its current limit at 0.3 A, then 0.1 A, into the DC jack through the solar INA228, the cell at about 3.7 V. Record whether PGOOD and CHG hold steady or cycle, and `charger_input_mv`.
