Your first Chicago bench prototype

Purchase the electronics, manufacture one small passive PCB, and print the supports. This build tests temperature/humidity readings and a direct radio link before we build outdoor hardware.

Prototype status: The custom PCB passed the recorded design-rule and netlist checks. The fixture CAD contains valid solids and closed meshes. Physical fit, wiring and firmware remain untested. These are indoor bench supports, not weatherproof outdoor enclosures. Purchased electronics in the render are simplified; cables are omitted.

Our custom PCBA 50 × 35 mm passive wiring adapter with probe points and a removable power jumper for measuring sensor current. It has no processor or radio. It makes bench testing easier; the initial test could also use jumper wires alone.
PCB / PCBAPCB: bare circuit board. PCBA: that board with components soldered onto it. Choose assembly if you want the headers already fitted.
Gerbers + drillsThe manufacturing drawings for copper, solder mask, labels, outline and holes. Upload their ZIP to the circuit-board factory.

STL / STEPSTL is a triangle model accepted by print services. STEP preserves solid CAD geometry for editing and services that accept it. These produce plastic parts, not electronic circuits.
LoRa / firmware / I²CLoRa carries the wireless data; firmware is the program loaded over USB into each board; I²C is the short wired connection between the sensor and transmitter.

2. Order the custom PCB with headers installed

Recommended route: PCBWay PCB quotation and assembly order. Read their assembly-file requirements if their upload form requests more information.

  1. Download and unzip chicago-test-hardware-r0.zip. Open its manufacturing folder.
  2. At PCBWay, sign in and start a PCB quote. Upload sensor-interface-gerbers.zip to the Gerber upload field. Do not upload the whole project archive into this field.
  3. Use the fabrication settings below. Check that the online preview has the right outline, headers, labels and four mounting holes.
  4. Enable Assembly Service; choose Turnkey (factory buys parts), Top side, and 5 individual assembled boards. This is through-hole assembly. There are no surface-mount parts or stencil.
  5. Attach BOM.csv, placement.csv, assembly-top.svg or PNG, and assembly-instructions.txt in the assembly/supporting-file fields. If the site takes one archive, use sensor-interface-assembly-quote.zip. Through-hole placement may be handled manually; the drawing governs orientation.
  6. Request file/BOM review before paying. Ask them to include and fit the removable JP1 shunt. Our board has three soldered headers and ten solder joints. The factory may need a panel or rails because one dimension is under 50 mm; ask them to create it and deliver separated boards. Do not scale the design.
  7. Review the final parts, assembly, shipping and tax quote, then complete checkout with your Chicago delivery address. Production begins after approval/payment under the service’s terms.

Bare-board alternative: solder it yourself

Open JLCPCB’s PCB upload/quote page. Select Add your Gerber file, upload sensor-interface-gerbers.zip, and use the same fabrication settings. Check the Gerber Viewer, select no assembly and no stencil, save to cart, then select review before payment if offered. Their order instructions describe the checkout flow.

For each bare board, buy 2 × Samtec TSW-104-07-G-S, 1 × TSW-102-07-G-S and 1 × SNT-100-BK-G shunt using Samtec’s buy/distributor options. Solder the ten header pins using the supplied drawing.

3. Order the 3D-printed supports

  1. Open PCBWay’s 3D-printing service and choose its online quote. Verify the process is 3D printing, not CNC machining.
  2. First upload mechanical/nucleo-fit-coupon.stl, quantity 1. Select FDM, PETG, millimeters, 100% scale, natural/white, standard finish, no paint or inserts. If unavailable, quote unfilled PA12 with SLS. Use the same process/material for the later cradles.
  3. Confirm the dimensions below in the preview. STL does not declare units, so millimeters matters. Request flat printing with the base on the build plate and removal of any supports.
  4. After the coupon arrives, check the real Nucleo width and underside-pin clearance. It should rest without forcing or loading the pins. Adjust CAD if necessary before ordering the full cradles.
  5. Upload the other three STL files separately with the quantities below. Review price, dimensional tolerance and delivery, then pay. STEP equivalents are included if the service prefers solid CAD.

4. Assembly and first test

The full package’s docs/build-and-order.md gives pin-by-pin wiring and checks; docs/bench-equipment.md contains the bench list. The PCB base uses four M3 × 12 mm screws, four M3 hex nuts, thin nylon washers and rubber feet at least 3 mm tall. The cradles are open supports, not locking mounts. Keep the sensor away from warm boards and unrestricted by tape or adhesive.

Connect using the assembly drawing and labels, checking power polarity first. Both Nucleo boards initially use USB power. Firmware implementation and a 24-hour end-to-end test are the next development steps; ordering these parts alone does not produce a working, programmed weather station.
