# Chicago Stage 0 — build and order instructions

Engineering prototype R0, 8 October 2026. This package implements the user's request for a test PCB and printed bench fixtures. The v0.2 staged architecture remains the authority for later subsystems. This fixture is an optional convenience for Stage 0; it is not a radio/controller PCB or the future RS-485 module interface.

## What to order first

1. Two ST NUCLEO-WL55JC1 boards, high-band variant, from ST's listed distributors (e.g. DigiKey or Mouser). These are already assembled and include ST-LINK and SMA antennas. Buy two Micro-B USB data cables with host ends matching the computer/hub.
2. One Adafruit SHT45 breakout, product 5665, plus one Adafruit 4397 JST-SH to individual female socket cable (150 mm). Buy four short male-to-female 2.54 mm jumper leads for Nucleo-to-J1, plus a few spares. Follow signal labels rather than assuming cable order or color.
3. Five copies of the passive sensor-interface PCB. Only one is required for the initial transmitter; the remainder are spare fixtures. Receiver needs no custom PCB at this stage.
4. One Nucleo fit coupon. Once it fits the actual revision, print two Nucleo cradles, one sensor-interface base and one sensor tray.
5. For the PCB base: four M3 x 12 mm screws, four standard M3 hex nuts, four thin nylon washers, and four adhesive rubber feet at least 3 mm high. Inspect screw protrusion; shorten or use thicker feet if needed. Use loose nylon ties or small removable foam dots to retain the separate breakout, away from its sensing aperture. No special crimping is needed.

The radio and sensor are purchased assembled modules. The custom PCB carries three through-hole headers and one removable shunt; it requires ten solder joints if ordered bare. No sensor chip reflow is required. Storage/backfill hardware and firmware from the Stage-0 plan remain separate work; this package does not silently claim H01 completion.

## Manufacturer selection

**Recommended turnkey route: PCBWay.** Upload the Gerber ZIP for fabrication and request through-hole assembly using BOM.csv, placement.csv, assembly-top.svg and these instructions. Their assembly service supports SMT and through-hole work. Ask them to procure the three Samtec headers, solder them on the top side, and fit the shunt after inspection. The shunt is an assembly accessory, not a fourth soldered component. Request a quote for five boards and confirm minimum quantity, component sourcing, any substitutions, shipping and import charges before paying.

**Lower-overhead route: JLCPCB bare boards**, then solder the three headers yourself. The proposed layout uses ordinary two-layer manufacturing. Assembly is optional; a bare-PCB order does not include parts.

**Printed fixtures:** PCBWay FDM PETG, or SLS unfilled PA12 if PETG is unavailable in the quote. JLC3DP is another quoting option. Send STL for printing; STEP is included for geometry review and editing. Never send the bare-PCB STEP file to a printer as a functional circuit board.

Manufacturer capability pages checked on 2026-10-08. No quote, purchase, upload to a manufacturer or fabrication order has been made. Do not infer price, lead time or stock from this design package.

## Wiring

Power off while wiring. J1 and J2 are unkeyed 2.54 mm headers; each pin 1 is square, at the top when the front text is upright. These headers deliberately do not share the JST-SH connector's physical pin ordering. Plug individual cable sockets according to this table.

| Fixture pin | Signal | Nucleo host connection | Sensor connection |
|---|---|---|---|
| J1.1 → JP1 → J2.1 | 3.3 V | CN6 pin 4 / 3V3 | Breakout VIN/3V, via cable power lead |
| J1.2 ↔ J2.2 | Ground | CN6 pin 6 / GND | GND |
| J1.3 ↔ J2.3 | SDA | CN5 pin 9 / D14 / PA11 / I2C2_SDA | SDA |
| J1.4 ↔ J2.4 | SCL | CN5 pin 10 / D15 / PA12 / I2C2_SCL | SCL |

Use the ST board labels and UM2592 Table 17 to identify pins; do not count from an assumed viewing direction. Configure I2C2 at 100 kHz, open-drain, MCU internal pullups disabled; the Adafruit breakout provides pullups. Sensor address is 0x44. Keep all I2C leads short (target ≤25 cm total host-to-sensor) and the sensor away from the warm MCU/regulator. This unbuffered I2C connection is for the bench only.

TP1=3V3_SENSOR, TP2=GND, TP3=SDA, TP4=SCL. These are exposed copper pads, not components to buy. Probe using insulated tips or the header pins. JP1 is normally shunted. Remove its shunt and insert a series ammeter to measure sensor-rail current; never put an ammeter between 3.3 V and ground. Removing the shunt is not a guaranteed isolation switch because signal pins can back-power an unpowered sensor. Disable I2C and make signal pins high impedance before intentionally removing sensor power. DMM burden voltage can disturb readings; use PPK2/appropriate instruments for dynamic measurements later.

## Printed fixtures

| File stem | Qty after fit check | Size (mm) | Function |
|---|---:|---|---|
| nucleo-fit-coupon | 1 first | 84 x 10 x 14 | Checks 70.8 mm cradle clearance and edge support against actual board |
| nucleo-open-cradle | 2 | 84 x 80 x 14 | Open desktop support; four short edge rests, clearance underneath |
| sensor-interface-base | 1 | 70 x 55 x 9 | Four 3.4 mm through holes and underside hex-nut pockets; PCB sits 9 mm above bottom |
| sensor-ventilated-tray | 1 | 42 x 38 x 6 | Raised slotted tray for loose sensor breakout; generic rather than guessed hole pattern |

Print in the supplied orientation with the flat underside on the bed, 0.20 mm layers, 4 perimeters and about 30% infill for PETG. No automatic scaling. The base nut-pocket roofs and tray vents require short bridges; inspect the slicer, use localized support if your printer cannot bridge them cleanly. PA12 SLS uses the service's own process settings. Do not use conductive/carbon-filled filament. The Nucleo cradle is open and unlatched: do not carry it by the PCB or antenna. Verify connector clearance and that no underside pins touch plastic before powering. Actual board revisions and print tolerances require physical fit checks.

These are indoor test fixtures, not weatherproof cases, radiation shields or accurate outdoor-temperature exposure hardware. Chicago placement tests should keep electronics dry and within development-board limits. Full outdoor shield/enclosure qualification remains Stage 4.

## Incoming inspection and first power

1. Compare bare board dimensions and drill pattern with the drawing. Inspect pad plating, solder bridges, connector orientation and solder quality under magnification.
2. With no host or sensor attached, verify the three signal/ground paths are continuous. With JP1 open, verify J1.1 does not connect to J2.1. Fit the shunt and verify that power path becomes continuous. Verify no cross-net shorts. Subtract lead resistance for low-ohm checks.
3. Keep Nucleo disconnected. Feed only J1.1/J1.2 from a bench supply set to 3.3 V and a 20 mA limit. Verify J2.1 is approximately 3.3 V with the shunt fitted; the empty passive fixture should draw no measurable steady current. Switch off.
4. Remove the bench supply completely. Connect the Nucleo and SHT45 according to the table. Power the Nucleo via USB with normal factory power jumpers. Never connect an independent bench supply to this rail while USB/Nucleo power is also feeding it.
5. Flash the Stage-0 firmware when available; check one sensor read, its CRC, timestamps and fault reporting before LoRa. No firmware is included in this hardware package.
6. At the final Chicago test positions, run the specified 24-hour H01 test and record distance, floors/walls, antenna orientation and data gaps. PCB manufacturing checks do not establish RF performance or weather accuracy.

## Validation and release state

- KiCad 7.0.11 generated the source, Gerbers, drills and placement file.
- DRC: zero violations, zero unconnected pads, zero footprint errors, with 0.25 mm minimum copper clearance / 0.50 mm edge clearance used in the scripted run.
- Exported schematic netlist and PCB both match the five intended nets, including the split sensor power rail. Header pitch and drill counts checked.
- The header hole size follows Samtec's published 1.02 mm recommended footprint.
- CadQuery 2.7 generated valid single-solid fixtures, editable STEP and STL output. Mesh checks are recorded in validation/mesh.json.
- Physical fit, assembly workmanship, electrical bench behavior, RF performance, outdoor durability and H01 have NOT been tested. This is a first-article prototype package for manufacturer DFM review and small-batch fabrication, not a qualified station release.
- KiCad 7 CLI does not provide schematic ERC. The passive schematic was netlist-checked; no ERC pass is claimed. The MCU, breakout and cables are external and must be verified when connected.
