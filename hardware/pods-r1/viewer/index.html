The UV dome crown

The UV pod as it sits on top of the radiation shield: a round drum with a smooth dome lid. The LTR390 hangs under the window at the apex. The lid twists on with a bayonet and seals on a radial O-ring. Four M3 screws come up through the cap's flat top into the drum's floor. Drag to turn it. Slide to take it apart.

Ø117.6 drumOuter radius 58.8, inner 54.0. The lid's skirt sits flush with the drum outside.
28 mm dome riseA two-spline profile over the spigot, 3.0 shell, apex flat at z 58.2 with the window recess Ø20.3.
Bayonet, 25° clockwiseThree lugs at 90°, 210° and 330°. Turn it clockwise seen from above to lock it, like a jar lid.
Radial O-ringA cord round the body's spigot, 7 mm below its top. The groove is 351.4 mm round with about 25% squeeze.
LTR390 under the windowBoard top at z 51.3 on two bosses, held by 2 × M2.5 × 6 into Ø2.1 pilots. Its lead drops into the drum.
4 × M3 × 10 from belowUp through the cap's counterbores into heat-set inserts in floor bosses on a 31 mm radius. The gland exits the drum side, away from the pole.

The LTR390's 4-core lead loops once round the drum from the board up to the sensor. Take it apart and the lead stretches out with the lid, so you can see how much slack it needs, and the window disc lifts out of its recess on top.

Everything here is our own CAD from hardware/pods-r1/box/podbox.py and the Session Q models. The shield and the remixed cap aren't drawn: the shield model's licence is unknown, so its mesh stays off pages. The screws hang where the cap's flat top would be.
