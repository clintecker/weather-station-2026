# Breadboard bench r1: wiring plan

Generated by `layout/outputs.py` from `build.py` (checked against `../schematic/model.py`) and
`routing.py`. Don't edit it by hand.

Hole names match the printing on the Elegoo boards. `BB1:h24` is board 1, row h, column 24. Rows a-e
are the upper half, top to bottom, and rows f-j are the lower half. Column 62 is at the left and column 1
is at the right. Plate positions are in mm from the back-left corner, with x to the right and y towards you.

Each rail hole gets a name from its job and side. `GND ↓52` is the blue - rail along the bottom edge,
straight below column 52. `3V3 ↑18` is the red + rail along the top edge, above column 18. Every + rail
is 3V3 and every - rail is logic ground.

[BB1](kicad/BB1-top.png) ([iso](kicad/BB1-iso.png)), [BB2](kicad/BB2-top.png) ([iso](kicad/BB2-iso.png)),
