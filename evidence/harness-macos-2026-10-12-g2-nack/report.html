PASS means the scenario's declared integrity/recovery expectations passed. It does not mean every scenario met 99% live delivery. Deliberate outages and clock faults retain their full denominator. Noisy-link scenarios measure delivery; the report does not assume their fault rates match real RF conditions.

Drain time is additional virtual time after sampling ends, with at most two queued observations attempted per minute. Real backfill still needs an airtime and receive-window budget.

What ran

Actual Python gateway, AES-CCM authentication, SQLite commit/ACK boundary, persistent transmitter queue, restart recovery, durable nonce allocator and freshness logic. Seeded network faults are injected after encryption.

Evidence

Each scenario directory contains scenario.json, result.json, trace.jsonl, availability.json and a committed observations.sqlite3 snapshot. The campaign summary is summary.json.

Limits

Simulation uses host SQLite and public test keys. It does not model RF physics, FCC configuration, duty cycle, firmware power loss mid-flash-write, buffer capacity/overflow, energy use, environmental effects, a live MQTT broker or Home Assistant. Never deploy the deterministic test keys.
